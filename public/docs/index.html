Free reads and paid calls

Basic lookups are free, because many providers give them away. Calls that do real work for you are paid, at 0.0022 SOL per call, from credits you add to your wallet.

Free reads FREE

Balance, latest blockhash, token holdings, recent transactions, token account address

  1. You send a requestNo key needed.
  2. The gateway checks and prepares itApplies a rate limit and fills in anything you left blank.
  3. Live data is fetched from SolanaNothing is stored or guessed.
  4. You get a clean answerStructured JSON.

Paid calls 0.0022 SOL

Transaction preview, pre-flight safety check, token safety report, priority fee advice, explain transaction

  1. You send a request with your keyHeader x-api-key.
  2. The gateway checks and prepares itConfirms your key has a credit and fills in anything you left blank.
  3. Live data is fetched and analyzedThe gateway does the interpretation for you.
  4. You get an answer and one credit is usedHeaders show what you have left.

About the pre-flight check. It tells you what it checked, for example whether the fee payer can cover the base fee, and shows the result of a dry run. It is a check for common mistakes, not a guarantee that a transaction will succeed.

Get a key and add credits

You only need a key for paid calls. There is no signup form and no email. You prove you own a Solana wallet, and the wallet is your account.

Step 1

Sign in with your wallet

Request a short message, sign it with your wallet, and send the signature back. Signing in is free and sends nothing to the network. You get an API key.

Step 2

Add credits

Send SOL from the same wallet to the gateway address. Credits are added automatically when the deposit is detected, at 0.0022 SOL per paid call.

Step 3

Make a call

Send your key in the x-api-key header. Responses show your remaining credits in the x-credits-remaining header.

curl "https://YOUR-GATEWAY/api/solana/token-profile?mint=<token_mint>" \
  -H "x-api-key: <your_key>"

Keys cannot be recovered, because only a hash is stored. Signing in again issues a new key and retires the old one. The exact commands and the deposit address are on the Pricing and Top-Up tab.
